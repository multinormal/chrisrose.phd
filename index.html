Statistician & researcher. Randomized trials & meta-analysis. Oslo, Norway.

Chris Rose is a senior statistician and researcher at the Norwegian Institute for Public Health (NIPH).

  • PhD in statistical modeling (University of Manchester, UK)
  • Royal Statistical Society Fellow and Chartered Statistician (CStat)
  • Over 25 years of biomedical research experience

Based in the Center for Epidemic Interventions Research, a WHO Collaborating Center at NIPH, he also contributes to the Cluster for Health and Social Care Interventions and the Cluster for Global Health. His current work includes:

  • Design and analysis of randomized trials of public health and social measures (PHSM) for infection control, educational interventions to improve health choices, and digital interventions for maternal and child health
  • Complex meta-analysis of social and welfare interventions to support national decision-making
  • Evaluation of artificial intelligence and machine learning tools for evidence synthesis

He offers statistical consulting and training within the institute, nationally, and internationally, specializing in explaining statistical methods in an accessible way to non-statisticians.

Previously, he worked on methods for analyzing imaging-based early-phase trials of novel cancer therapies, and on neoantigen prediction for personalized oncoimmunotherapy.
